# 🎉 Ghoomo Travel Planner - Beta Release Guide

## Welcome Beta Testers!

Thank you for helping us test Ghoomo! This guide will help you get started and provide feedback.

### Core Features
- ✅ **AI Trip Planning** - Generate personalized itineraries
- ✅ **Flight & Hotel Search** - Find the best deals with Amadeus API
- ✅ **Trip Management** - Save and organize your trips
- ✅ **Weather Integration** - Check destination weather

- ✅ **Supabase Backend** - Faster, more reliable database

- ✅ **Settings Screen** - Complete preferences management

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up
1. Open the app
2. Tap "Sign Up"
3. Enter your name, email, and password
4. Check your email for verification (if enabled)

### 2. Create Your First Trip
1. Tap a destination card on home screen
2. Select your travel persona
3. Choose dates and budget
4. Let AI generate your itinerary

### 3. Search Flights & Hotels
1. Tap the search card on home screen
2. Enter your travel details
3. Browse results
4. Tap "Book Now" to reserve

## 🐛 Known Issues

1. **Windows Desktop** - Some Firebase features may not work (use web version)
2. **Email Verification** - May take a few minutes to arrive
3. **Search Results** - Limited to mock data if Amadeus API quota exceeded
4. **Offline Mode** - Limited functionality without internet
